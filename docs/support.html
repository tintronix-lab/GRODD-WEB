Help with your pot

Most problems are one of four things. If none of these is yours, write to us — a person answers.

Email: tintronix@gmail.com

Tell us the pot's serial if you have it — it is the GRODD-XXXXXX code on the base, and it is also shown in the app under the pot's Technical info.

The app cannot find my pot during setup

A pot announces itself over Bluetooth for a while after it is switched on, then goes quiet to save power. One short press of the button wakes it up again and makes it visible.

Keep the phone next to the pot while it sets up, and hold the button only briefly — a ten-second hold is the factory reset, which erases the pot's setup instead.

My pot is not watering

The pot decides this for itself, and the app says which reason applies. The common ones:

What the app saysWhat to do
The reservoir is emptyRefill it. The pot will not run a pump against an empty tank.
No level readingThe water sensor has not been calibrated yet — do it from the pot's settings.
The soil is wet enoughNothing. It is working.
A fault is latchedThe pot watered and the soil did not respond, so it stopped rather than keep pumping. Check the tube and the pump, then clear the fault in the app.

These limits live in the pot itself, not in the app, so they hold even when the pot is offline. The cloud can make them stricter and never looser.

My pot has gone quiet

A pot on mains reports every minute or two. A pot on battery wakes about every fifteen minutes and is unreachable in between — that is normal and not a fault. If it has been silent far longer than that, check power first, then that it is still in range of your Wi-Fi.

Updating a pot

Updates arrive by themselves if you leave automatic updates on, usually within a few minutes of a release. You can also start one from the pot's settings. An update downloads, verifies itself on the pot, restarts, and is only kept once the pot has checked in on the new version — if it cannot, the pot returns to the version it had.

Starting a pot over

Holding the pot's button for ten seconds erases its setup — Wi-Fi, calibration and configuration — and returns it to setup mode so it can be claimed again. Calibration is not restorable, so each channel has to be calibrated again afterwards.
