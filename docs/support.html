Help with your pot

Almost everything that goes wrong is one of four things, and your pot will have told the app which. Start there — then write to us if it is none of them.

Talk to a person

tintronix@gmail.com — a person answers, not a form.

Include the pot's serial if you can: the GRODD-XXXXXX code on its base, also shown in the app under the pot's Technical info.

The app cannot find my pot

A pot announces itself over Bluetooth for a while after switching on, then goes quiet to save power. It is not lost — it is asleep.

  1. 1Find the small button on the pot.
  2. 2Give it one short press — a tap, not a hold.
  3. 3Keep the phone beside the pot while it sets up.

Keep the press brief. Holding the same button for ten seconds is the factory reset, which erases the pot's setup instead of waking it.

My pot is not watering

The pot decides this for itself and tells the app which reason applied. These limits live in the pot's own firmware, not in the cloud, so they hold even when it is offline — and the cloud can make them stricter, never looser.

The reservoir is empty
Refill it. A pot will not run its pump against an empty tank.
No level reading
The water sensor has not been calibrated yet. Do it from the pot's settings — it takes two readings, empty and full.
The soil is wet enough
Nothing to do. This is the pot working.
A fault is latched
The pot watered, the soil did not respond, and it stopped rather than keep pumping into a blockage or a spill. Check the tube and the pump, then clear the fault in the app.

My pot has gone quiet

Plugged in — reports every minute or two
Silence longer than about five minutes means unplugged or off the network.
On battery — wakes about every fifteen minutes
Unreachable in between, and that is the design rather than a fault. It is how a battery lasts.

If a pot has been silent far longer than its own rhythm, check power first, then that it is still within reach of your Wi-Fi.

Updating a pot

Updates arrive by themselves if automatic updates are on, usually within a few minutes of a release. You can also start one from the pot's settings.

An update downloads, the pot verifies it itself, restarts, and is only kept once the pot has checked in on the new version. If it cannot, the pot puts the old version back on its own — there is no state where an update leaves a pot half-installed.

Starting a pot over

Holding the button for ten seconds erases the pot's Wi-Fi, calibration and configuration and returns it to setup mode, ready to be claimed again.

Calibration does not come back. Each channel has to be calibrated again afterwards, and until it is, the pot will not claim to measure what it cannot.
